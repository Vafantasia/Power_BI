# 📊 Sales Dashboard — Historical Sales Analysis

### 📌 Overview

This repository contains a **Sales Dashboard** developed to analyze sales performance and compare results across previous years.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key sales indicators, allowing users to identify **performance trends, year-over-year variations, sales evolution, and business patterns**.

The main objective is to transform raw sales data into clear and actionable information to support **business analysis and decision-making**.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

* Analyze sales performance over different periods.
* Compare current results with previous years.
* Identify year-over-year growth and declines.
* Monitor key sales performance indicators.
* Analyze sales trends over time.
* Identify the main contributors to sales performance.
* Facilitate the interpretation of historical sales data.
* Provide an interactive and intuitive reporting environment.

---

## 📈 Key Analyses

The dashboard focuses on several dimensions of sales performance, including:

* Total Sales
* Sales evolution over time
* Year-over-year comparison
* Sales growth
* Monthly and yearly performance
* Product performance
* Customer performance
* Sales distribution
* Historical trends
* Variations between periods

The analyses can be explored interactively through filters and visualizations available in the dashboard.

---

## 🗓️ Historical Analysis

One of the main purposes of this dashboard is to provide a **historical perspective on sales performance**.

By comparing different years, it becomes possible to identify:

* Positive and negative growth trends.
* Changes in monthly sales patterns.
* Periods of higher or lower performance.
* Recurring seasonal patterns.
* Significant variations between years.
* Products or customers contributing to performance changes.

Historical comparisons help provide context to current results and make it easier to understand whether a variation represents a short-term fluctuation or a broader business trend.
